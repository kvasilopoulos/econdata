The data behind the papers, ready to load.

32 datasets from influential empirical macro papers, curated as an R package and published as plain CSV so they work from any language. Each one keeps the sample and variables the authors used.

sw2001 <- read.csv("https://kvasilopoulos.github.io/econdata/data/sw2001.csv")
sw2001$date <- as.Date(sw2001$date)
import pandas as pd
gk2015 = pd.read_csv("https://kvasilopoulos.github.io/econdata/data/gk2015.csv", parse_dates=["date"])
import delimited "https://kvasilopoulos.github.io/econdata/data/rz2018.csv", clear
pak::pak("kvasilopoulos/econdata")
library(econdata)
catalog      # what is in the package

Static API

Everything on this page is a file you can fetch. There is no server and nothing to authenticate; pin a commit on GitHub if you need a frozen copy.

Find other replication data

Freely available datasets behind commonly replicated papers that are not bundled here: too large, updated regularly, or restrictively licensed. Author websites are the best source for shock series because authors keep extending them.

Ramey (2016) Handbook of Macroeconomics data — Single zip with monetary, fiscal, technology and uncertainty shock series plus the Stata/Matlab code used in the Handbook chapter. Also hosts Ramey-Zubairy (2018) military news shocks.
Used in: Ramey (2016); Romer and Romer (2004); Christiano, Eichenbaum and Evans (1999); Coibion (2012); Ramey (2011). monthly/quarterly, 1889-2015.

Romer and Romer (2004) monetary policy shocks — Narrative shocks from Greenbook forecasts. Updated series to 2007 (Coibion et al. 2017) and 2019 (Wieland and Yang) circulate; the Ramey zip bundles one.
Used in: Romer and Romer (2004); Coibion (2012); Ramey (2016). monthly, 1969-1996.
Gertler and Karadi (2015) high-frequency surprises — Replication package on the AEA site. Extended surprise series are maintained by Jarocinski and Karadi (2020) and by Bauer and Swanson (2023).
Used in: Gertler and Karadi (2015); Ramey (2016); Jarocinski and Karadi (2020). monthly, 1990-2012.
Jarocinski and Karadi (2020) monetary and information shocks — Poor man's sign restrictions split of high-frequency surprises. AEA replication package.
Used in: Jarocinski and Karadi (2020). monthly, 1990-2019.
Bauer and Swanson (2023) monetary policy surprises — Orthogonalised FOMC announcement surprises; updated on the author's site.
Used in: Bauer and Swanson (2023). monthly, 1988-2023.
Miranda-Agrippino and Ricco (2021) informationally-robust shocks — Monetary policy shock series purged of central bank information effects.
Used in: Miranda-Agrippino and Ricco (2021). monthly, 1991-2009.
Wu and Xia (2016) shadow federal funds rate — Drop-in policy rate for VARs spanning the zero lower bound.
Used in: Wu and Xia (2016). monthly, 1990-present.

Kilian (2009) oil market VAR data — World oil production, global real activity index, real oil price. The activity index is maintained by the Dallas Fed (IGREA).
Used in: Kilian (2009); Kilian and Murphy (2014); Baumeister and Hamilton (2019). monthly, 1973-2007.
Kilian global real economic activity index — Corrected and updated by the Dallas Fed; download as xlsx.
Used in: Kilian (2009); Kilian and Zhou (2018). monthly, 1968-present.

FRED-MD / FRED-QD — Drop-in replacement for the Stock-Watson large panels (e.g. the 120 series in bbe2005). Monthly vintages are archived so results can be pinned to a date.
Used in: McCracken and Ng (2016); Stock and Watson (2016); Bernanke, Boivin and Eliasz (2005) updates. monthly/quarterly, 1959-present.

Gilchrist and Zakrajsek (2012) excess bond premium — Updated monthly by the Fed Board (ebp_csv.csv). This is the `ebp` column in gk2015.
Used in: Gilchrist and Zakrajsek (2012); Gertler and Karadi (2015). monthly, 1973-present.

Mertens and Ravn (2013) narrative tax shocks — Personal and corporate income tax narrative measures used as external instruments.
Used in: Mertens and Ravn (2013); Mertens and Ravn (2014). quarterly, 1950-2006.
Auerbach and Gorodnichenko (2012) fiscal multipliers — AEA replication package with forecasts from SPF and Greenbook.
Used in: Auerbach and Gorodnichenko (2012). quarterly, 1947-2008.

Fernald utilization-adjusted TFP — Updated quarterly by the San Francisco Fed.
Used in: Fernald (2014); Basu, Fernald and Kimball (2006); Gali (1999) updates. quarterly, 1947-present.

Jurado, Ludvigson and Ng (2015) uncertainty indexes — Macro, real and financial uncertainty at 1, 3 and 12 month horizons; updated regularly.
Used in: Jurado, Ludvigson and Ng (2015); Ludvigson, Ma and Ng (2021). monthly, 1960-present.
Economic Policy Uncertainty index — US and country-level indexes plus categorical sub-indexes.
Used in: Baker, Bloom and Davis (2016). monthly/daily, 1985-present.

Shiller online data (S&P, CAPE, house prices) — The long S&P price/dividend/earnings series behind psy2015 and most bubble tests.
Used in: Shiller (1981); Campbell and Shiller (1988); Phillips, Shi and Yu (2015); Case and Shiller (2003). monthly/annual, 1871-present.

Jorda-Schularick-Taylor Macrohistory Database — 18 advanced economies, 1870 onwards. CC BY-NC-SA licence; download as xlsx or dta.
Used in: Schularick and Taylor (2012); Jorda, Schularick and Taylor (2017); Jorda et al. (2019). annual, 1870-present.

Reinhart and Rogoff crisis chronologies — Country-level crisis dates and debt series.
Used in: Reinhart and Rogoff (2009); Reinhart and Rogoff (2011). annual, 1800-present.
Laeven and Valencia systemic banking crises database — IMF WP 18/206 with the database as an appendix xlsx.
Used in: Laeven and Valencia (2013); Laeven and Valencia (2020). annual, 1970-2017.

Penn World Table — Also on CRAN as the `pwt10` package.
Used in: Mankiw, Romer and Weil (1992); Hall and Jones (1999); Feenstra, Inklaar and Timmer (2015). annual, 1950-present.
Maddison Project Database — Long-run GDP per capita. CRAN: `maddison` package.
Used in: Bolt and van Zanden (2020). annual, 1-2018.
Barro-Lee educational attainment — Download as csv or dta from the project site.
Used in: Barro and Lee (2013). 5-yearly, 1950-2015.

Acemoglu, Johnson and Robinson (2001) settler mortality — Data archive on Acemoglu's MIT page covers most of his published papers.
Used in: Acemoglu, Johnson and Robinson (2001); Albouy (2012). cross-section, 1995.

Nelson and Plosser (1982) US macro series — Bundled in R: `tseries::NelPlo`. Extended to 1988 by Schotman and van Dijk (1991).
Used in: Nelson and Plosser (1982); Perron (1989); Zivot and Andrews (1992). annual, 1860-1970.

Lutkepohl (2005) and JMulTi textbook datasets — Bundled in R: `vars::Canada`; e1-e6 datasets ship with JMulTi and `urca`.
Used in: Lutkepohl (2005); Pfaff (2008). quarterly, 1960-2000.
Kilian and Lutkepohl (2017) textbook datasets — The `kl2017` and `oil` datasets in this package come from here; the site has the full chapter-by-chapter data and Matlab code.
Used in: Kilian and Lutkepohl (2017). quarterly, 1954-2013.

AEA Data and Code Repository (openICPSR) — Mandatory replication packages for all AEA journals. Search by DOI.
Used in: Any AER, AEJ, JEP or JEL paper since 2019. various, various.
Journal of Applied Econometrics Data Archive — Long-running archive; most classic applied time-series papers (Hamilton, Perron, Pesaran...) have entries.
Used in: Any JAE paper since 1988. various, various.
Harvard Dataverse — Many top-5 journals deposit replication packages here; search by paper title.
Used in: QJE, ReStud, JPE, Econometrica replication packages. various, various.
Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) — R access via `fredr` or `quantmod::getSymbols(src = 'FRED')`. ALFRED holds real-time vintages.
Used in: Nearly every US macro paper. various, various.
