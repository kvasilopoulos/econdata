## Notes for the integrator

- All 11 objects (`rr2017`, `hkm2017_quarterly`, `hkm2017_monthly`, `bw2006`, `gwz2024_monthly`, `gwz2024_quarterly`, `gwz2024_annual`, `rr2009`, `rf2009`, `fr2009`, `nr2020`) build cleanly from `Rscript data-raw/datasets/finance.R` and were spot-checked against their raw source files (e.g. US financial distress = 14 at 2008Q3 in rr2017, matching the Lehman-crisis peak; SENT = 0.72 in Jan 2000 in bw2006, matching the dot-com peak).
- Categories used: Crises (rr2017, rr2009), Financial conditions (hkm2017_*), Asset prices (bw2006, gwz2024_*), Labour (rf2009), Business cycles (fr2009, nr2020) - all from the allowed list, no new category needed.
- Licence/attribution: all eight sources are free-to-use academic replication/data pages with no login or NC/ND restriction found; `rr2009` is a secondary reconstruction (PERI/Herndon-Ash-Pollin) of Reinhart-Rogoff's data rather than the original authors' own posted file, since their site blocks scripted access - flagged in the catalog notes and roxygen in case the integrator wants to try reinhartandrogoff.com again by hand.
- `gwz2024` predictor mnemonics had slashes (`d/p`, `d/y`, `e/p`, `d/e`, `b/m`) which were stripped to `dp`, `dy`, `ep`, `de`, `bm` for valid R column names (this is also how most downstream users refer to them).
- Key collisions checked against `data-raw/catalog/core.csv` and `data/`: none of `rr2017`, `hkm2017_quarterly`, `hkm2017_monthly`, `bw2006`, `gwz2024_monthly`, `gwz2024_quarterly`, `gwz2024_annual`, `rr2009`, `rf2009`, `fr2009`, `nr2020` were already used.
